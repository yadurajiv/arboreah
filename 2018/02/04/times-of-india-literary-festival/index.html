Times of India, Literary festival

Writing

As the head of studies for the creative writing project, we released our literary publication 'Unselves' at the Times of India Literary festival, Bengaluru 2015. Unselves puts together a collection of stories around the theme of identity and the body.  The book release was done by Tishani Joshi, an eminent poet,  in the presence of the students of Creative Writing students of Srishti Institute of Art, Design, and Technology.
